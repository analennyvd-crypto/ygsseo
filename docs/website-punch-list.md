# YGS Brothers — Website Punch List

Site audited: ygshandymanservices.com (Squarespace)
Date: 2026-08-18

Full interactive version: published as a Claude artifact and shared with the client in chat.

## Diagnosis

Google Business Profile shows a 5.0★ rating across 7 reviews but only 4 customer
interactions — the reputation is strong, but almost nothing is converting that
into site traffic or calls. Two root causes:

1. **Trust signals are broken** — empty testimonial placeholders, several blank
   image blocks, no phone number visible outside the Contact page.
2. **Local SEO is essentially absent** — no page titles/descriptions, no H1 with
   a service area, only one of the ten served cities named anywhere on the site.

## Part 1 — Trust & Conversion

| # | Fix | Priority |
|---|-----|----------|
| 1 | Remove/replace the 4 empty testimonial placeholder boxes | Critical |
| 2 | Add a "Call Now" button (`tel:+17865168575`) to the site header | Critical |
| 3 | Lighten body-section backgrounds; keep black/gold for hero + logo only | High |
| 4 | Upload real work photos (bathroom remodel, garage floor, plumbing, drywall) into every blank placeholder | High |
| 5 | Remove the email newsletter signup on the Contact page | Quick win |

## Part 2 — Local SEO
