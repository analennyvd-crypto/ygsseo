# YGS Brothers — Lead Pipeline (Marketing Playbook)

Full interactive version: published as a Claude artifact and shared with the client in chat.
Companion file: `templates/lead-tracker-template.xlsx`.

## Baseline

- 5.0★ on Google, 7 reviews, but only 4 customer interactions in the last 30 days —
  the gap is visibility, not reputation.
- 90-day target: 25+ Google reviews via steady weekly posting across all three channels.

## Cadence

| Frequency | Action |
|---|---|
| Per job | Send a Google review request text the same day |
| 2×/week | Post to Google Business Profile (Mon & Thu) |
| 3×/week | Post to Facebook groups, rotating 2 groups each time, reworded each post |
| Weekly | Refresh/repost the Craigslist ad (Wednesday), reworded |
| Weekly | Fill in the lead tracker |

## Google Business Profile

- Primary category: Handyman. Add secondary categories: Construction Company,
  Remodeler, Painter, Plumber, Electrician.
- List all 8 services and all 10 service-area cities individually.
- Turn on GBP messaging.
- Post 2×/week (recent work / service spotlight / availability), always with a photo.
- Upload 2–3 photos from every completed job.

## Facebook Groups

- Join 6–8 active local groups per service city ("[City] Community", "[City] Buy Sell
  Trade", county classifieds groups). Read each group's posting rules first.
- Post 3×/week, rotating groups and rewording each post to avoid spam flags.
- Mix ad posts with genuine engagement (answering questions, commenting) to build
  trust and avoid getting muted.

## Craigslist

- Post under the local Tampa Bay Craigslist site → Services → Skilled Trade Services.
- Rewrite the title and first line each week rather than reposting identical text.
